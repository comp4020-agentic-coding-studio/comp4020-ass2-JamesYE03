Tomas came to this course sideways, from a research career on Frankfurt's
philosophy of bullshit, and now spends most of his time arguing that
"synthetic media forensics" is really applied epistemology wearing a lab coat.
He teaches the two weeks most people expect to be the driest — the arms race
in week 8, and the ethics of accusation in week 11 — and has made peace with
being wrong about that expectation every year.
