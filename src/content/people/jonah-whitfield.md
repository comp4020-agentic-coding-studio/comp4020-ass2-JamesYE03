Jonah spent several years drafting platform content policy before moving to
teaching, which he describes as trading one impossible-to-satisfy audience for
several younger ones. He teaches week 7's introduction to C2PA and week 10's
platform policy session, and keeps a running, only half-joking list of the
edge cases every real disclosure policy he's ever read got wrong.
