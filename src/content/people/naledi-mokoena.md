Naledi trained as a librarian before this field had a name, running
information-literacy workshops for a public library system. She teaches weeks
2 and 3 — SIFT and textual tells — and holds the class to a rule she learned
long before generative AI existed: if you can't say where a claim came from,
you don't get to repeat it yet.
