Ravi builds detection tooling for a living and teaches its limits for fun,
which he insists are the same job done twice. He covers weeks 4 through 6 —
images, video and watermarking — and is the tutor most likely to tell a
confident student that their detector's "99% accuracy" claim is measuring the
wrong thing.
