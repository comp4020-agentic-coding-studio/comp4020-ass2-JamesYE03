Elena spent a decade as an investigative reporter before the newsroom's
research desk started asking her to fact-check its own writers' AI-assisted
drafts. She teaches week 9's workslop session and mentors the week 12 case
presentations, where her only fixed piece of feedback is the same one every
year: a report that isn't legible to a stranger isn't finished yet.
