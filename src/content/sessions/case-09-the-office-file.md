Every document in this session is fictional, and every one of them is modelled
on a completely ordinary piece of office writing — a status update, a
one-pager, a meeting summary — because workslop's whole trick is that it
never looks extraordinary.

## Before the session

Nothing to prepare. Bring whatever patience you have left for reading
plausible-sounding paragraphs that go nowhere; you'll need it.

## In the session

Read three short fictional workplace documents and, for each, decide: does
this actually advance the task, or does it just look like it does? Then write
one sentence of feedback you could actually send to the (fictional) author
without accusing them of anything you can't support.

## Afterwards

The skill this session is really testing is restraint — naming a real problem
with the artefact while staying scrupulously fair to the person who produced
it. You'll need both halves of that in any real workplace, this course's
casework included.
