Twelve weeks of casework end with your own case, chosen by you, argued to a
standard nobody had to tell you to meet by the end.

## Before the session

Have a five-minute version of your Assignment 4 investigation ready to
present, distinct from the full written report — a courtroom summary, not a
recitation of your working notes.

## In the session

Presentations, in short blocks with real questions from the room. You are
being asked what any investigator is asked at the end of a case: what did you
find, how sure are you, and what would change your mind.

## Afterwards

Assignment 4 is due at the end of the semester, not today — today is the
dry run. Use the room's questions to find the part of your report that isn't
as solid as you thought it was, while there's still time to fix it.
