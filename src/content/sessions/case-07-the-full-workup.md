**Assignment 2 is due today.** This is the hinge session of the course: the
last one built entirely on inferring from an artefact, before week 7's lecture
introduces the idea of a record that doesn't require inference at all.

## Before the session

Submit Assignment 2 before the session starts.

## In the session

A short peer-review round: swap reports with someone else in the room and
check one thing only — does their stated confidence level actually match the
evidence they present, or did their conclusion get ahead of their proof? This
is the single most common failure mode in real forensic writing, and it's
worth catching in each other's work before your marker catches it in yours.

## Afterwards

Half the course is behind you. From here, the emphasis shifts from "can you
run each method" to "can you tell when a method has reached the end of what
it can support."
