**Assignment 3 is due today.** This is the most adversarial session of the
course, on purpose: you were assigned a side of a genuine provenance dispute
in week 10, and today you find out how it holds up against someone assigned
the other one.

## Before the session

Submit Assignment 3 before the session starts.

## In the session

Paired cross-examinations: each of you spends five minutes attacking the
other's case, using nothing but questions. No new evidence is allowed —
only pressure on the evidence already presented. The point isn't to win; it's
to find out which parts of your case were actually load-bearing.

## Afterwards

Whatever survived the cross-examination is what you're entitled to still
believe. Anything that didn't survive is worth revisiting before week 12,
where the same discipline gets applied to a case you chose yourself, with
nobody assigning you a side to defend.
