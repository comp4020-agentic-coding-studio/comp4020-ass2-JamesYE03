You were asked to bring one suspect: a text, an image, a clip you already
half-believe is synthetic. This session doesn't ask you to prove it. It asks
you to notice exactly what your suspicion is actually made of.

## Before the session

Bring your suspect artefact and one sentence describing why you doubt it. Not
a paragraph — a sentence. If you can't compress your reason to one sentence,
that's information too.

## In the session

Short show-and-tells, then a group sort: which suspicions rest on something
checkable (a date, a source, a claimed author) and which rest on vibes ("it
just feels off"). Vibes aren't worthless — they're often the first sign
something is wrong — but they are not where a case ends.

## Afterwards

You leave with your artefact still unproven and a short list of what you'd
need to check to actually close the case. Weeks 2 through 7 hand you that
list, one tool at a time.
