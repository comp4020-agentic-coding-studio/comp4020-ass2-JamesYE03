Video and audio are where the gap between "there's a check for that in
principle" and "I can run that check on this specific ten-second clip, today"
opens widest. This session is about learning to see that gap honestly instead
of pretending it isn't there.

## Before the session

Watch the two case studies linked from this week's lecture before you arrive.
You don't need to have an opinion yet — you need to have watched them.

## In the session

Given a short clip and a claim about it, you'll draft a checklist of every
method covered so far that could plausibly apply, then actually attempt each
one and mark it done, inconclusive, or not applicable with the tools
available to you.

## Afterwards

The gap between your checklist and what you could actually complete is the
most honest measurement of your current forensic reach. Expect it to be
bigger than you'd like.
