Playing adversary for a session is uncomfortable if you've spent seven weeks
building up the other set of skills. It's also the fastest way to understand
exactly how much confidence any single detector actually deserves.

## Before the session

Nothing to prepare beyond having read the week's lecture — you'll be working
with material provided in the session.

## In the session

Given a piece of AI-generated content and a detector, you have twenty minutes
to make edits that push the detector's confidence down without materially
changing what a human reader would take away from the content. Log every
change you tried, including the ones that didn't work.

## Afterwards

The honest version of this exercise ends with most of the room succeeding,
at least partially. Sit with what that means for any policy — including one
you might draft in week 10 — that treats a detector's verdict as sufficient
grounds for a decision on its own.
