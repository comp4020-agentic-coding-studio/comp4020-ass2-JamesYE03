**Assignment 1 is due today.** This session is half clinic, half trial: bring
your workup, then swap with someone who ran the same passage through
different checks than you did.

## Before the session

Submit Assignment 1 before the session starts. Bring a printed or on-screen
copy of your own workup — you'll need to walk someone else through it without
narrating your Google Doc over their shoulder.

## In the session

Pair up with someone whose verdict differs from yours on at least one case
from the practice set. Each of you has three minutes to argue your reading.
The rest of the room votes, then we look at what the room actually knew
versus assumed.

## Afterwards

The votes aren't your grade — your submitted workup is. But a room that
confidently votes for the wrong verdict is exactly the failure mode this
course exists to prevent, and it's worth sitting with before week 4 raises the
stakes to images.
