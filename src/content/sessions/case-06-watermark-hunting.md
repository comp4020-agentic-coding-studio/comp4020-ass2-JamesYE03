You are not attacking anyone else's system today — you're attacking your own,
which is both safer and more honest, because you get to see exactly what
survived and exactly why.

## Before the session

Nothing to prepare. Bring a device that can screenshot and re-save an image at
least twice.

## In the session

Embed a toy watermark in an image using the week's provided tool, then put it
through a gauntlet: screenshot it, recompress it, crop it, re-upload it
through a messaging app to yourself. Check after each step whether the
watermark still reads.

## Afterwards

Compare notes with the room. If everyone's watermark died at the same step,
that's not a coincidence — that's a specific, nameable weakness worth
carrying into week 7's discussion of provenance.
