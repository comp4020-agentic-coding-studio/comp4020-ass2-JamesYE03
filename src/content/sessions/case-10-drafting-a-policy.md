Reading someone else's policy for its gaps is a different skill from reading
content for its tells, and it's one this course has been building toward all
along: knowing exactly how far a rule reaches, and naming precisely where it
stops.

## Before the session

Read the real platform policy linked from this week's lecture before you
arrive, and bring one case you think it handles badly.

## In the session

In small groups, pick the single worst gap the room found and draft one
clause that closes it, without accidentally closing off the legitimate uses
(satire, research, consented synthetic likeness) that week 10's lecture
flagged as the cases every current policy gets wrong.

## Afterwards

Post your clause to the discussion thread. The best ones from across the room
become the discussion material for week 11, where you'll have to argue against
a policy position you don't actually hold.
