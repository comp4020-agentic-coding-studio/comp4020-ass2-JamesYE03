Bring a laptop. This is the first fully hands-on session, and the tooling
matters — you can't reason your way to a verdict about pixel-level artefacts
you haven't actually looked at.

## Before the session

Nothing to prepare. Everything you need is provided in the session, on
purpose: this is where "I would have checked that" turns into actually
checking it.

## In the session

Working through a set of five images — some real, some synthetic, some real
photos recirculated with false context — you'll run a reverse search and a
detector on each and log where they agree, where they don't, and which one
you'd trust more for that particular kind of forgery.

## Afterwards

Keep your log. Assignment 2, due in week 7, asks you to combine methods across
weeks 3 to 7 on a mixed-media case, and today's disagreement-tracking habit is
exactly the discipline that assignment rewards.
