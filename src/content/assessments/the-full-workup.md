## The brief

> Given a case involving more than one kind of media, work it the way the
> first seven weeks taught you to: multiple methods, checked against each
> other, written up so someone else could follow your reasoning and disagree
> with a specific step if they wanted to.

Assignment 1 asked for two checks on one kind of content. This asks for at
least three, spanning at least two of text, image, video and provenance
checking — and it asks you to notice, in writing, where your methods agreed
and where they didn't. A response that runs three checks that all point the
same way and stops there is doing less work than one that runs three checks,
finds a genuine disagreement between two of them, and reasons through it.

## What you submit

A forensic report (800–1200 words, images or screenshots welcome and not
counted against the length): the case, every method applied and what each
found, an account of any disagreement between methods, and a verdict with a
stated confidence level that a reader could sanity-check against the evidence
you presented.
