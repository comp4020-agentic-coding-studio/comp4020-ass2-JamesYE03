## The brief

> Take one passage of text you don't already know the origin of, and work out
> — with evidence, not instinct — whether a person or a model most likely
> wrote it.

This is the lowest-stakes piece of graded work in the course and it is meant
to be: a single passage, not a mixed case; a written verdict, not a live
presentation; two checks, not a full toolkit. The room this assignment leaves
is in the choice of passage and the choice of checks — a strong response finds
a genuinely hard case rather than an easy one, and picks two checks that could
actually disagree with each other rather than two that always agree.

## What you submit

A short written workup (300–500 words is plenty): the passage or a link to
it, the two checks you ran, what each one told you, and your verdict with a
stated confidence level. Show your working — a bare verdict with no visible
reasoning is not a workup, it's a guess with a byline.
