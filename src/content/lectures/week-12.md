No single method from this course is sufficient on its own, and by now that
shouldn't be a disappointing conclusion — it should be the whole point.
Textual tells decay. Watermarks get stripped. Provenance standards only cover
what was signed in the first place. Detectors get gamed the moment they
matter. What holds up is the practice underneath all of it: treat a claim as a
case, gather more than one kind of evidence, know exactly how confident each
piece of evidence entitles you to be, and never let the accusation outrun the
proof.

That practice was never really about AI. It's the same discipline Caulfield
taught for misinformation and Bergstrom and West taught for statistical
bullshit, aimed at a harder adversary because the adversary got faster. It
will need to be re-aimed again in a year, at whatever comes after this. That's
not a flaw in the method. It's the reason the method, and not any single
tool inside it, is what you're actually taking with you.
