## Case one: the Hong Kong video call

- early 2024, a finance employee joins a video call with several "colleagues,"
  including the CFO
- instructed to transfer roughly USD 25 million across multiple transactions
- every participant except the employee was a synthetic reconstruction
- no research-grade model required — commodity tooling was enough

---

## Case two: the crypto endorsement

- a deepfake video impersonating a well-known entrepreneur
- used to promote a cryptocurrency scheme
- convinced an elderly retiree to invest his life savings
- the fake didn't need to fool an expert — it only needed to fool one person,
  once

---

## Synthesis vs reenactment

- **synthesis** — building a face or voice largely from a model's learned
  distribution
- **reenactment** — puppeting a real, consenting or non-consenting person's
  likeness with someone else's performance
- different techniques, different artefacts, different defences

---

## What a post-hoc review can and can't do

- **can**: check compression artefacts, audio-visual sync, lighting
  consistency, metadata trails where they still exist
- **can't**: fully undo what a lossy re-encode already destroyed, or prove a
  negative from absence of evidence alone
