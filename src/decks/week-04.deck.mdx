# Pixels Don't Lie (Usually)

## Week 4 — image forensics

---

## Two long histories collided

- **old**: retouched, staged and misattributed photographs — reverse image
  search, EXIF, shadow-and-light consistency
- **new**: diffusion models hallucinate an image from nothing, leaving a
  different kind of trace entirely

---

{/* _class: impact */}

# Most fakes aren't generated. They're recirculated.

---

## The cheap checks first

- reverse image search — has this exact image appeared before, with a
  different caption or date?
- metadata (EXIF) — camera, timestamp, edit history, when it survives
  re-upload
- these catch a shocking amount, because most misinformation is a real photo
  misdated, not a synthetic one

---

## What a diffusion model leaves behind

- irregularities in frequency space invisible to the eye
- inconsistent reflections and shadows across a scene
- anatomical tells — hands, teeth, text-within-the-image — that are getting
  rarer every model generation

---

{/* _class: quote */}

"The hands look wrong" was never a durable method.
