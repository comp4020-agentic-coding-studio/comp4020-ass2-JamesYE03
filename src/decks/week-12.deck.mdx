# No single method here was ever sufficient alone.

---

## That's not a disappointing conclusion

- textual tells decay the moment they're published
- watermarks get stripped by ordinary handling, let alone a hostile one
- provenance standards only cover what was signed in the first place
- detectors get gamed exactly in proportion to how much weight rests on them

What holds up is the practice underneath all four: treat a claim as a case.

---

## The actual throughline

Frankfurt → *Calling Bullshit* → *AI Snake Oil* → this course. Same discipline,
aimed at a faster adversary each time. It will need re-aiming again next year,
at whatever comes after generative slop. That's not a flaw in the method.
