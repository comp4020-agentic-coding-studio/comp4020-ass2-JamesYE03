# The world is awash in slop.

---

## A short lineage

- 2005 — Harry Frankfurt, *On Bullshit*: a liar cares what's true; a bullshitter
  doesn't
- 2020 — Bergstrom & West build a course, then a book, out of that essay
- 2023 — a Princeton computer scientist calls a chatbot "a bullshit generator"
  and means it precisely
- **now** — the bullshit is manufactured at machine scale, by systems with no
  stake in the truth at all

---

## What actually changed

- **scale** — one person, then; a data centre, now
- **confidence** — slop arrives looking exactly as polished as the real thing
- **intent** — nobody upstream necessarily meant to deceive anyone

None of that makes it less of a problem. It makes it a different one.

---

{/* _class: quote */}

A liar knows the truth and hides it. Neither the model nor most of what it
generates knows or cares what the truth is at all — that's the harder case.

---

## The scale, in numbers

- AI-generated content detected online is estimated to have grown **550%**
  since 2023
- Google's own research suggested up to **40%** of web content could be
  AI-generated by the end of 2025

Neither number needs to be exact to make the point: this is not a niche
problem anymore.

---

## The forensic stance

- evidence over instinct
- more than one method, checked against each other
- a stated confidence level, never louder than the evidence earns
- a chain of custody: who made this, and what happened to it since
